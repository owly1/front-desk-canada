# Demo company and policy knowledge

Grand River Plumbing & Heating is fictional. Three illustrative technicians: Marc, Sofia, Devon. Cities: Waterloo, Kitchener, Cambridge, Guelph. No real service, licences, insurance, operating hours or emergency availability is represented.

Services eligible for local demo appointments: water_heater, drain_clog, leak_repair, toilet_faucet, maintenance_tuneup. A two-hour appointment is a demo scheduling window, not a completion-duration promise. Bathroom renovation, other work and emergencies require a person; no price is assigned automatically.

Prices come exclusively from get_quote. They are illustrative CAD ranges, not validated market prices or binding offers. Scope, final price, taxes and extras require assessment. No blanket free-service-call promise, discount, deposit or after-hours fee is represented.

The calendar is local synthetic availability for three business days, in America/Toronto. No Google Calendar, Jobber or field-service platform synchronization exists. Booking confirms this demo calendar only.

SMS is preview-only by default. Live mode requires configured Twilio credentials, a consenting test-recipient allowlist and explicit SMS consent. The template labels the appointment a demo. No payment link exists. Outbound callbacks and transfers are not implemented.

The owner dashboard is English. Initial language candidates are English, French and Mandarin; model and human QA determine what may be advertised. No universal fluency or Quebec compliance claim.

Caller recording/transcription policies must be disclosed and consented to. Provider settings need separate verification. Use fictional data only. Do not retain a transcript in this backend when no consented intake exists; provider retention is independently controlled.
