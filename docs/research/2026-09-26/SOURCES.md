## Most important unresolved questions

1. **Demand and buyer:** do enough reachable operators have recurring, valuable language-mismatched conversations and authority to pay for a better workflow?
2. **Incremental benefit:** does shared confirmation and continuity improve real task outcomes enough to beat an existing translator, bilingual colleague or business product?
3. **Delivery economics:** can the complete service be provided reliably with acceptable support, integration and language-evaluation costs at the proposed price?

Secondary gaps: exact event acceptance of concept-only sponsor entries; preferred initial pair; booking-system access; noisy-room and phone performance; interruption/recovery behavior; actual billed character/audio volumes; customer acquisition costs; seasonal retention; and privacy/retention arrangements agreed with operators. These are production/discovery questions, not reasons to limit the ambition of a clearly identified concept film.
