# Demo runbook and pitch guardrails

## What can be demonstrated now

The local owner workflow and labelled synthetic scenarios. Voice/telephony are account-configuration gates, not completed milestones. Never edit a video to suggest a synthetic scenario was a live phone call.

## Local rehearsal

1. Launch with a fresh DB_PATH; open the owner dashboard.
2. Create a fictional manual inquiry with storage consent; explain that an owner can review the same data the agent would submit.
3. Select an available slot. Read back details and check explicit confirmation. Confirm and reload; the booking must persist.
4. Generate an SMS preview; show PREVIEW status, not a sent toast.
5. Try the French/Mandarin-labelled scenarios. State that these exercise backend handling, not speech quality.
6. Try the renovation scenario: no invented C$8,000 value. Show human review.
7. Try an emergency: no regular booking, no dispatch or promised response time.
8. Open Call receipts: it remains empty until an actual signed provider receipt arrives. This is intentional evidence separation.

## Target 90–120-second video after live configuration

- 0–15s: A small service team is busy doing the work; a caller needs help in a language the available team may not speak. State this as the workflow being tested, not a quantified national fact.
- 15–25s: Canada has over a million small employer businesses (ISED December 2024 data); start with one Waterloo home-service workflow, not every SME.
- 25–65s: One real consented conversation → range → confirmed slot → owner handoff. Include a supported-language switch only after QA.
- 65–85s: Inspect actual booking and message state. Label local calendar, fictional business, and estimated value.
- 85–105s: Existing AI receptionists are real competitors. Proposed pilot compares successful bookings, total owner effort, errors and costs on the same kinds of inquiries.
- 105–120s: Ask for introductions to test the hypothesis. C$149/month is proposed pricing, not a proven profitable plan. No guaranteed Fellowship, endorsement or customer claim.

Luma currently permits up to five minutes. Preserve a short cut and confirm whether organizers changed the limit. Live panel is after submission, not inside the original brief's final build-hour schedule.

## Growth case

Our ambition is a service economy where a small team can serve more people with less coordination overhead. The immediate test is time saved and correctly handled inquiries. A lead transferred from a competitor is not automatically new Canadian GDP. Any national projection must expose assumptions and avoid double-counting.

## Tough questions

**Why not Jobber?** It already answers and books. We must demonstrate that our narrower multilingual setup/handoff serves a specific operator better. This is not yet established.

**Why hasn't AI solved it?** Parts are solved. Our test concerns domain/language quality, reliable actions and adoption cost—not a claim that voice AI appeared eighteen months ago.

**How much revenue recovered?** We have estimated booked value and open pipeline. Realized revenue and causal recovery require later observation and a comparison.

**Can it speak Punjabi?** Only if the chosen model supports it and the team validates quality; not on the default Flash v2.5 assumption. Advertise tested languages only.

**What is real?** Database actions, validated booking, owner UI and authenticated adapters. Fictional business and local calendar. Provider calls/SMS only after the integration gates are actually passed. No payments or emergency dispatch.
