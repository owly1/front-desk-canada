Evidence before claims.

Our hypothesis: a multilingual front desk can reduce coordination work for small service teams. We measure inquiries and estimated bookings—not invented GDP, recovered revenue, or guaranteed business outcomes.

  • Caller confirmation before booking.
  • Consent before storing contact details or sending SMS.
  • Model interprets; server validates price and availability.
  • Urgency is flagged for people. No emergency dispatch or response-time promise.
  • Existing AI receptionists are the benchmark to beat.
